# 05 · Report Standard

[中文](../05-报告规范.md) | English

## Core structure

A research report keeps these responsibilities visible:

- question and scope;
- sources and evidence quality;
- analysis and comparison;
- conflicts, gaps, and unknowns;
- conclusions and decision boundaries;
- source list and method appendix.

## Citations

Use stable references such as `[^s-0001]`, `[^f-0001]`, and `[^c-0001]`. Every citation must resolve to an envelope, fact, or conflict record.

## Neutral language

Do not present the author's preference as a finding. Report facts, inference, and symmetric options separately.

## Numbers

Always preserve unit, period, denominator, source, and calculation method. Derived numbers must be reproducible.

## Machine checks

Validate citation resolution, required metadata, status consistency, and report-to-ledger alignment. Machine checks supplement rather than replace substantive review.
