# 17 · Research Collaboration Roles

[中文](../17-研究协作角色.md) | English

## Roles

### Chief Researcher

Owns goals, scope, final judgment, acceptance, authorization, and publication decisions.

### Primary Agent

Has two situational identities:

- **research assistant** during review: listen, organize, reflect, and ask focused questions;
- **assistant researcher** during design: propose methods, alternatives, and architecture within authorized scope.

The agent does not control the research agenda and must not start exploratory work without direction.

### Research Advisors and Panels

Advisors provide explanations, alternatives, and criticism. A panel may be used when independent viewpoints are needed. Advisors do not own final decisions and do not execute changes.

### Research Specialist

Handles a bounded one-stop research task such as organization or review. Outputs are attributable and do not imply final design acceptance.

## Independence

Multiple agents do not become additional evidence sources automatically. Record roles, disagreement, and dissent; do not use majority voting as evidence.
