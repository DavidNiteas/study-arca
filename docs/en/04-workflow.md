# 04 · Research Workflow

[中文](../04-工作流.md) | English

## Five domains

1. **Scope**: define the question, user, decision context, range, and exclusions.
2. **Collect**: build coverage, retrieve sources, and store envelopes and raw responses.
3. **Analyze**: extract evidence, build argument bridges, identify conflicts, and separate inference from observation.
4. **Discuss**: return findings to the user, capture feedback, corrections, and follow-up tasks.
5. **Present**: create audience-specific output from the current evidence state.

Discussion may reopen collection, analysis, scope, or presentation. The process is iterative, not a one-way waterfall.

## Round closure

A round closes when:

- the current question has a defensible answer or explicit unknown state;
- claims have sources or are marked unresolved;
- feedback and follow-up work are recorded;
- the evidence state and presentation state are distinguishable.

Closing a round does not mean publishing or that all uncertainty is resolved.
