# 13 · Research Analysis and Argument

[中文](../13-研究分析与论证.md) | English

## Minimum argument

For each claim identify:

- question;
- intervention or exposure;
- control or comparison;
- observed outcome;
- mechanism or reasoning bridge;
- boundary and alternative explanation.

A citation proves provenance, not the validity of the interpretation.

## Reading papers

Separate material/method, intervention, comparison, measured outcome, statistical result, author interpretation, and limitations. Do not treat an abstract conclusion as the whole experiment.

## Synthesis across sources

Preserve agreement, disagreement, context differences, and missing evidence. A cross-source conclusion must explain why evidence from different settings can be combined.

## Output semantics

Report, appendix, and PPT may differ in density and order. They may not change the factual layer, hide critical unknowns, or present speculative mechanisms as verified.
