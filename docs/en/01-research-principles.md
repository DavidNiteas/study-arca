# 01 · Research Principles

[中文](../01-调研治理.md) | English

## Goal

Produce traceable, reviewable research that separates evidence, inference, unknown state, and optional recommendations.

## Invariants

1. Raw evidence is append-only and never silently rewritten.
2. Claims must point to identifiable sources or explicitly remain hypotheses.
3. The final decision belongs to the user or client; the agent organizes evidence and options.

## Neutrality

- Keep `fact`, `inference`, and `option` distinct.
- Do not use directive wording such as “recommended”, “should”, “must”, “priority”, “do not do”, or “Go/No-Go” in external research material unless the user explicitly asks for a decision memo.
- Alternatives must be symmetric in scope, conditions, resources, risks, and disconfirming evidence.
- Charts and titles must not encode a preference that the evidence does not establish.

## Quality

- Every material claim can be traced to a source envelope.
- Conflicts and missing evidence remain visible.
- Quantitative statements preserve period, denominator, unit, and comparison baseline.
- A clean lint does not replace substantive research judgment.

## Review

Before delivery, perform a subjectivity review: inspect wording, comparisons, rankings, recommendations, and visual emphasis. Keep self-review and independent review distinct.
