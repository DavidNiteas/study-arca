# 08 · Research Toolbox

[中文](../08-调研军火库.md) | English

This document catalogs optional search, crawling, academic, news, and vertical-domain MCP tools.

## Selection

Choose tools by source coverage, reliability, access constraints, licensing, and the evidence grade required. A more specialized tool is not automatically better evidence.

## Compliance boundary

Crawlers and high-risk platforms are discovery aids. Public reports must cite the original publisher or authoritative record, not the scraper alone.

## Maintenance

Update capability status when a connector changes. Record authentication, quotas, known failures, and fallback path.
