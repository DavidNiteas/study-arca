# 09 · Research Loop

[中文](../09-专业调研循环.md) | English

## Work domains

`scope → collect → analyze → discuss → present`

The domains may run concurrently. Discussion can trigger more collection, re-analysis, scope changes, or presentation revisions.

## Records

`research/cycle.json` is the append-oriented source for agreements, questions, rounds, evidence actions, discussion feedback, follow-ups, and presentation bindings.

## Inputs

User-provided materials enter through `brief/inbox/` or an explicit path, then are registered by `sa input add` into `brief/provided/` with role, authority, verification state, and hash.

## Freezing

Research artifacts and Research IR snapshots have stable IDs and hashes. Formal outputs consume frozen snapshots, not mutable chat state.

## Quality

A round requires explicit unknowns, traceable claims, conflict handling, and a clear boundary between evidence and inference.
